# pixelarticons

Eight unmodified glyphs from [pixelarticons](https://github.com/halfmage/pixelarticons) by Gerrit Halfmann, version 2.4.2 ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/pixelarticons/v/2.4.2)), MIT licence (see `LICENSE`, copied from the package).

`scripts/pixel_badges.py` inlines them into the Pixel arcade achievement badges and sets only their colour and size.
